Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. We handle your data responsibly and securely.

*Effective Date: November 9, 2025

Information We Collect

We collect information primarily to process your orders and improve your shopping experience. This data includes:

  • **Personal Identification Data:** Name, email address, phone number, and shipping address (collected during checkout).
  • **Transactional Data:** Products purchased, order total, and payment details (payment processed securely by third-party gateways; we do not store your full card details).
  • **Usage Data:** IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the website (collected via Google Analytics to improve site performance).

How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used for the following primary purposes:

  • To **process and fulfill your orders** efficiently.
  • To **communicate with you** regarding your order status, shipping updates, and customer service requests.
  • To send **marketing and promotional emails** (only if you opt-in) about new products or offers.
  • To monitor and analyze usage to **improve our website and product offerings**.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share data with essential third parties necessary to run our business:

  • **Shipping Partners:** To fulfill delivery of your order (Name, Address, Phone).
  • **Payment Gateways:** To process payments securely (Transactional data).
  • **Analytics Providers (e.g., Google):** For website performance monitoring (Usage data).

Security and Your Rights

We implement robust security measures to protect your data. You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in the email.
