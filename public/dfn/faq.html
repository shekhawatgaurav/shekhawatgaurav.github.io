Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to our most common questions about quality, orders, and delivery.

What does "Cold Pressed" truly mean at DFN?

At DFN (Direct From Nature), 'Cold Pressed' means the oil is extracted using a slow-grinding process where the temperature never exceeds **50Â°C (122Â°F)**. This preserves the natural aroma, flavour, antioxidants, and essential fatty acids, unlike refined oils which use high heat and chemicals like Hexane.

Are your oils filtered or refined?

No. Our oils are **unfiltered and unrefined**. We rely on natural sedimentation over several days to remove solid particles. You may notice natural sediments at the bottom of the bottle; this is a sign of purity and natural processing.

What is the best way to store DFN oils?

Store the oil in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight and heat. Keep the cap tightly closed after use. Our oils typically have a shelf life of 12 months from the date of bottling.

How long does delivery take?

We typically process and ship orders within 1-2 business days. Delivery usually takes **3-7 business days** depending on your location in India. You will receive a tracking link via email once your order is dispatched.

What is your return/refund policy?

Due to the nature of our food products, we cannot accept returns once the seal is broken. However, if you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact us within **48 hours** of delivery with photos, and we will arrange a replacement or full refund.
