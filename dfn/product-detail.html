The DFN Sunflower Difference

Our Sunflower Oil is known for its light flavour and high smoking point, making it the perfect everyday cooking oil. It is pressed meticulously to ensure the natural profile of Vitamin E and essential fatty acids remains intact, providing maximum health benefits without compromising on taste.
