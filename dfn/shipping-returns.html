How do I check the status of my order?

When your order has shipped, you will receive an email notification from us which will include a tracking number you can use to check its status. Please allow 48 hours for the tracking information to become available.

Return and Refund Policy

Returns Due to Product Nature

Due to the perishable nature of our cold-pressed edible oils, we have a strict no-return policy for opened or partially used products. We cannot accept returns if you simply change your mind or dislike the flavor, as purity and hygiene are our top priority.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

We guarantee the quality and packaging of our oils. If your product arrives damaged, leaked, or if you receive the wrong item, we will happily replace it or issue a full refund.

  • **Eligibility:** You must notify us within **48 hours** of delivery.
  • **Requirement:** Please send clear photographic evidence of the damage to **support@dfnoils.com** with your order number.
  • **Resolution:** Upon verification, we will initiate a replacement order or a full refund to your original payment method.

Refund Processing

Refunds are typically processed within **5-7 business days** after approval and reflect in your bank account or credit card statement depending on your bank's policy.
